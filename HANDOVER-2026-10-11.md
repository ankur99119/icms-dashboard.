## 3. Verified ICMS facts — do not re-derive, do not break

These were established against live ICMS and cost real effort. Several are
counter-intuitive and a rewrite will silently lose them.

**Report 19 (PAM/19, Originating/Terminating, `action=OriginatingTerminating`)**
- Native measure is `right_time / reported`. This is the **originating**
  basis only.
- Identities that must hold: `scheduled − exceptions = eligible`;
  `reported + not_reported = eligible`; `right_time + late = reported`;
  the delay slabs sum to `late`; station rows sum to the division total.
- **It cannot be ranged.** `fromDate != toDate` returns one aggregate for the
  span. `native_summary` deliberately skips any capture where
  `fromDate != toDate`. A year is 365 separate requests.

**Report 16 (PAM/16, All Detention Details, `action=AllDetentionDetail`)**
- Form `frmAllDetention`; header in `#TABLE_2`, data in `#rpttbl`; submit
  `input[value="Display"]`. Controls: `reportFormat` S/R, `caseType` ALL/LT
  (pinned to LT), `divisionCode` DLI, `fromDate`/`toDate`,
  `currentDateFlag2`, `trainTypeMultiGroup`.
- Grain is the **event**, not the train. Continuation rows carry neither Sr.
  nor Train.
- **Zone / Division / Terminating Delay are TRAIN-level and repeat verbatim
  on every event row of that train.** Summing them across event rows gives
  11,827 minutes against a true 7,229 — a 64% overstatement. Only the `Time`
  column is per-event. This is why they are stored once per train in
  `detention_train_days` and named `train_level_*` on the event rows.
- **Sub-code identity is parent cause + sub-code.** `REPER_P` appeared under
  ACP, PATH and CNST in a single day. The sub-code alone is ambiguous.
- Each train sits under a `Primary Marked on: <CAUSE>` banner and has exactly
  one `Type=P` row whose Code equals that banner — 189 of 189 on the verified
  capture.
- The header carries `Total Delayed Trains: N`, which **must** reconcile
  against the parsed train count or the capture is rejected.
- **Detention Date is not the report date.** A 10-Oct report returned events
  dated 10, 09, 08 and 07 Oct.
- **It does take a real range.** Verified live on 08–10 Oct 2026: 973 trains,
  1,415 events, 1,459 rows, one request.
- **A wider window finds more events for the same date.** 249 events dated
  10-Oct from the three-day range against 234 from the single-day report. A
  date is therefore not settled until a window extending past it is captured,
  and overlapping captures must de-duplicate on `event_key`.
- Type takes P, N and S. P is the primary. **The meaning of N and S has never
  been established** — they are stored verbatim and not interpreted. Ask the
  user; he has not answered.

**Report 14 (O/P/T Point Performance) — the dual PP rule**
Verified against ICMS's own printed `pp_pct` column on six rows:
- at an **originating** point: punctual = RT + BT
- at a **terminating** point: punctual = RT + BT + MKUP + NLT + (1–15 min late)

Over the same five days that is **27.0% against 49.2%**. Most of that gap is
the rule, not the operation. Report 14 has no parser yet.

## 4. Invariants the code enforces — breaking one breaks the figures

1. **Never sum `train_level_zone_delay`, `train_level_division_delay` or
   `train_level_terminating_delay` across event rows.** 64% overstatement.
2. **De-duplicate on `event_key`** across overlapping captures.
   `detention_events.event_key` is the primary key and replay runs in
   `imported_at` order, so the newest capture of an event wins.
3. **`Total Delayed Trains` must reconcile** or the capture is refused.
4. **Exactly one `Type=P` row per train**, and its Code must equal its banner.
5. **Never average an incomplete day in with complete ones** (see §5).
6. **Sub-code is always qualified by its parent cause.**
7. **`right_time / reported` is the originating basis**; it is not the
   Report 14 PP% and the column is named `rt_percent` to keep them apart.

`tests/test_pam16.py` asserts 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 directly, including one test
that asserts the naive sum is *wrong* (120 against a true 40), and one that
asserts the indexed SQL path and the blob-scanning reference produce
identical output. `tests/test_sheets_export.py` asserts 5.

## 5. The completeness measure — a correction made this session

The dashboard previously marked a day provisional if `reported / eligible`
fell below half. **That misses a midday capture**, because a short capture
has a correspondingly small `eligible`.

Measured on 11 Oct from `data/icms.db`:

| date | stations | eligible | reported | right_time | rt% | coverage | complete |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-04 | 27 | 319 | 318 | 158 | 49.69 | 0.94 | yes |
| 2026-10-05 | 29 | 360 | 358 | 187 | 52.23 | 1.06 | yes |
| 2026-10-06 | 28 | 346 | 345 | 166 | 48.12 | 1.02 | yes |
| 2026-10-07 | 28 | 344 | 341 | 165 | 48.39 | 1.02 | yes |
| 2026-10-08 | 29 | 345 | 344 | 169 | 49.13 | 1.02 | yes |
| 2026-10-09 | 28 | 334 | 332 | 167 | 50.30 | 0.99 | yes |
| 2026-10-10 | 26 | 183 | 174 | 114 | **65.52** | 0.54 | **no** |
| 2026-10-11 | 27 | 326 | 3 | 0 | 0.00 | 0.96 | **no** |

10-Oct reads as the best day of the week and is half a day. It passed the old
test at 174/183. The measure now in `icms/sheets_export.py:day_completeness`
applies **both** tests: the day's `eligible` against the window **median**
(complete at ≥ 0.8), and `reported / eligible` (≥ 0.8). The verdict and the
ratio are written into the Sheet so the page and the CSV cannot disagree.

Six complete days sit between 48.12% and 52.23%.

## 8. Gotchas that cost time — do not rediscover these

- **`device_commit_files` reports success without writing.** Patch files with
  a shell heredoc or a Python read-modify-write instead, and verify.
- Shell commands over ~19 KB fail with `E2BIG`. Split them.
- `\uXXXX` escapes in a tool-call JSON body are decoded before reaching the
  shell, so they land in the file as literal characters. Scan for stray
  non-ASCII after any heredoc write.
- `pkill -f app.py` and `pgrep -f 'python3 app[.]py'` match the agent's own
  shell and kill it (exit 143). Never use broad process patterns.
- ICMS renderer freezes / CDP timeouts on heavy range queries. Do not retry
  in a loop.
- `Store.initialise()` does not exist; the constructor runs the schema.
- `Settings` has `db_path`, not `database`.
- A module global captured by a **default argument** is bound at import time.
  `EVENTS_WINDOW_DAYS` was read that way and `--events` silently did nothing.
- A `getElementById` placeholder scheme that restarts its counter per render
  lets a hidden view's stale element win the lookup. Chart ids must be
  monotonic.
- An SVG scaled by the browser scales its text: the same chart read 10px in a
  half-width panel and 24px in a full-width one. Charts must be drawn at
  their measured pixel width.
- `node --test tests/` fails on this Node; use `node --test tests/*.test.mjs`.
